# concepts

## forecasting
column: deals.products
synonyms: forecasting deal, forecasting customer, forecasting product
Forecasting is sold under the SKU name Horizon: match products ILIKE '%Horizon%'. The word forecasting in deal_name or notes is not the product.

## account_owner
column: accounts.account_owner
synonyms: account owner, who owns, owner of
Read accounts.account_owner. It is already the right person for customers and prospects.
